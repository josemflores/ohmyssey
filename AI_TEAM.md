# AI Team Log

A short log of how people and AI tools built Ohmyssey, for the Cursor + Grok Bot prize track.

## 2026-11-14 — Plan and scaffold

- **Jarvis (Grok Bot)** set the plan: an Odyssey-themed progressive electronics game, six islands from a closed loop on the beach at Troy to a 555 lighthouse beacon at Ithaca, with a data-driven level format and an Athena hint API.
- **Cursor cloud agent** built this starter: the Vite + React app, the voyage map, the slot circuit builder, playable Levels 1 and 2, stub Levels 3–6, and the Athena serverless function.
